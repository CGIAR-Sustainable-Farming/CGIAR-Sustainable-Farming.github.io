## Dataset inclusion criteria

Resources should support sustainable farming research, carry traceable source
evidence and clear reuse terms, and have a responsible source contact.
Supporting resources from other programmes keep their original attribution.

## Review process

Metadata begin in `drafts/` in the
[SFP catalogue repository](https://github.com/CGIAR-Sustainable-Farming/catalog).
Resolve missing facts, verify licensing authority and source links, and validate
against the shared CDH standard before promoting a record into `records/`
through a reviewed pull request. Structural validation does not establish
scientific accuracy or permission to redistribute data.

Named catalogue stewardship and final approval responsibilities still require
confirmation. No inherited CDH committee or reviewer arrangement is asserted as
SFP policy.

Draft updates never automatically promote a dataset. Daily website refreshes
publish the approved catalogue and link separately to review drafts. Failed
checks leave the previous site online.
